Runge-Kutta 4th Order (RK4)

Coupled 2nd-order vehicle motion ODE:

dx/dt = v / √(1 + (f'(x))²)

dv/dt = (F_drive + F_gravity + F_drag + F_roll) / m

Evaluates 4 stages (k₁, k₂, k₃, k₄) per step. Global truncation error: O(Δt⁴).

Euler Method
